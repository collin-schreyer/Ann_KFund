# K Fund walkthrough for Ann

Live app: https://ann-kfund.onrender.com/

## Opening

“This prototype reads receipt lines, checks for possible concerns against the supplied FY 2026 Domestic Representation Guidelines, shows supporting pages, and records a human reviewer’s decision.”

The receipts and event below are fictional. The guidelines are the user-provided FY 2026 document; confirm their applicability before using findings for an actual event. The prototype does not issue payments, approve reimbursements, or connect to EDCS records.

## Five to seven minute walkthrough

1. Choose **Start an event review** for a clean intake. Avoid loading pre-entered sample expenses and extracting the same receipts together.
2. Enter `Ann demo — fictional visiting delegates reception`, date `2026-04-15`, location `United States, outside New York City`, purpose `Fictional reception for visiting delegates`, foreign guests `4`, total guests `10`, and currency `USD`. Leave the optional comparison amount blank. Expand **Additional case details** to show the fields informed by Ann’s screenshots. Foreign guests and non-USG guests are distinct.
3. Continue to expenses and expand **Try a sample document**. Preview **Photographed catering receipt**, then **Extract sample**. Wait for the visible completion notice. Check its four lines: appetizers 760, beverages 240, service staff 320, delivery/setup 120; total 1,440.
4. Extract **Photographed venue receipt**. Check room rental 900, linens 240, decorations 85, setup service 150. There should now be **eight lines totaling 2,815**. Neither photo separately prints a tip. Extraction is editable and requires verification.
5. Optional separate tip illustration: add a manual check with a verified food/beverage base of 100 and tip of 20, choose Tip, and tick both verification boxes. **Check tip** gives within the comparison. Change the tip to 20.01, confirm it again, and check: above the comparison. Editing clears the previous confirmation. Remove this manual illustration before reviewing the receipts so it cannot be mistaken for a charge on them. Service charges and unknown/overseas context require review.
6. Choose **Analyze event** and wait for the draft. Show the decorations concern and its supporting PDF pages. Other charges can need context, approvals or follow-up. Every amount stays pending human review. The displayed 281.50 per guest is a simple total/guest calculation, not a certified reimbursement ceiling.
7. For Decorations, choose **Needs follow-up** and enter `Demo: verify the decorations exception and required approvals.` Save the shared demo review, then download the HTML report. Show the reviewer note, original receipt filenames, source pages and pending amounts. Reopen the saved case from Overview.
8. Open **Guidance search** and ask `What documentation is needed for reimbursement?`. Inspect a cited passage, then ask a follow-up. This uses the supplied FY 2026 document too.

## Presentation notes

- Allow time for live AI extraction/review; output wording can vary. Check totals and citations rather than memorizing exact wording.
- Do not describe a draft flag or a tip comparison as an automatic approval or rejection.
- The separately supplied London receipt is an older overseas receipt. Its service charge requires the appropriate date/location rules rather than a blanket domestic cap.
- Saved cloud cases are visible to demo users and can disappear on restart. Original receipt files are not retained with the case; keep the sample files available.
- Translation, currency conversion, real vendor/payment records, official approval routing and EDCS integration are follow-up work.
